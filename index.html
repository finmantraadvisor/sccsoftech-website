Common questions

Are classes live or recorded?

Classes are live online, so you can ask questions as you learn. Session recordings are shared for revision.

I am a beginner with no IT background. Which course should I start with?

Start with Networking Fundamentals or Operating Systems, or choose the 1-year Diploma in Cloud, which begins from the basics. Book a free demo and we will suggest a path based on your background.

Do you provide placement?

We provide interview preparation, resume support and job assistance, including referrals to openings we hear about. We do not promise a guaranteed job, because hiring decisions are made by employers.

Is the certification exam fee included?

The official exam fee (AWS, Microsoft, Cisco, EC-Council, Oracle and others) is paid to the certification body and is separate from the course fee. We guide you through registration and preparation.

Can I pay the course fee in instalments?

Yes, instalment options are available for longer courses and career programs. Ask us for details.

What do I need to rent a laptop?

A valid ID, a refundable security deposit and the delivery address. Companies can rent on a purchase order. Contact us with your quantity and duration for a quote.
